OrbitInfluences 1.0.0 adds SOI boundaries and predicted transition waves to KSP 1.12.5's Flight Map View and Tracking Station.

Choose an orbit-aligned ring or body-fixed spherical grid. Entry waves expand, exit waves contract, and maneuver plans can contribute future crossings. Use the in-game panel to toggle and color these features; preferences persist between sessions. The mod does not alter orbital mechanics and needs no other mod.

**Install:** Extract the ZIP's GameData/OrbitInfluences directory into your KSP GameData directory, then restart KSP. See the [README](https://github.com/Wid4er/KSP-OrbitInfluences#readme) for controls and build instructions.

The KSP assembly build and 46 isolated trajectory and geometry checks pass. Some visual combinations and modded planetary systems have not yet been validated in game.
